Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Kindle The Collective is a small, independent creative project that publishes narrated films, music and soundscapes under the channel names Kindle The Spark, Kindle The Cadence and Kindle The Calm. This policy explains what information we handle and why.

This website

This website is a static page. It does not use cookies, advertising, analytics or tracking tools, and it has no forms or accounts. We do not collect personal information from visitors. Our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) may keep ordinary server logs, such as IP addresses, under its own privacy policy. The latest-video pictures on the channel pages are loaded from YouTube's image servers (i.ytimg.com), so your browser contacts YouTube to show them; that is covered by the Google Privacy Policy.

Our publishing tools

We use software that we run ourselves to schedule and publish our own videos, clips and pictures to our own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, Facebook and TikTok. To do this, we connect only accounts that we own and operate, by signing in through each platform's official authorization screen.

  • What we receive: an access token for each of our own accounts, plus the basic account details the platform shows (account name and picture) and, when we ask for it, performance figures for our own posts (such as views and likes).
  • What we do with it: upload and schedule our own content, and read how our own posts perform. Nothing else.
  • Where it is kept: on private equipment we control, encrypted where the system supports it. Tokens are never published or shared.
  • What we do not do: sell, rent or share this information; use it for advertising; collect information about other people's accounts; or post on anyone else's behalf.

We follow the developer policies of each platform we connect to. Information obtained through YouTube's API services is also governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the YouTube Terms of Service. Instagram and Facebook are operated by Meta, and TikTok is operated by TikTok; their own privacy policies apply to their services.

Comments and messages on our channels

If you comment on or message one of our channels, the platform holds that content under its own terms. We may read and reply to it. We do not copy your comments elsewhere, and we do not use them to build profiles of you.

Children

Our content is made for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data deletion

Because this website collects nothing from visitors, there is nothing of yours to delete here. If you believe we hold any information about you from one of our channels, such as a message you sent us, contact us at the address below and we will delete it within 30 days. You can also remove our access to your own account at any time from that platform's connected-apps settings.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version on this page with a new date.

Contact
